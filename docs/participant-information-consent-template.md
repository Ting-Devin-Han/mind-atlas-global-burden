# Participant information and electronic consent / 参与者信息与电子知情同意模板

This document is a study-team template and must be reviewed and approved by the responsible ethics committee before recruitment.

本文档为研究团队模板，必须在招募参与者前由负责的伦理审查委员会审核批准。

## Required study details / 必须补充的研究信息

- Full study title / 研究全称
- Principal investigator and institution / 主要研究者及机构
- Research contact email and telephone / 研究联系邮箱与电话
- Ethics committee and approval reference / 伦理委员会及批准编号
- Funding and conflicts of interest / 资助与利益冲突
- Recruitment period and target sample / 招募时间与目标样本量
- Data controller, database location and cross-border transfer status / 数据控制者、数据库所在地及跨境传输情况
- Retention period and deletion date / 保存期限与删除日期
- Complaint and withdrawal contact / 投诉与撤回联系渠道

## Consent statements / 同意事项

Participants must actively confirm that they:

参与者必须主动确认：

1. are aged 18 years or older / 已年满18周岁；
2. have read and understood the participant information / 已阅读并理解参与者信息；
3. understand that sensitive mental-health and socioeconomic information will be processed for the approved research purpose / 理解将为经批准的研究目的处理敏感心理健康和社会经济信息；
4. understand foreseeable discomfort, confidentiality protections, retention and publication arrangements / 理解可能不适、保密措施、保存期限与发表方式；
5. participate voluntarily and may leave before submission without penalty / 自愿参与并可在提交前无责任退出；
6. know how to contact the research team and ethics committee / 知道如何联系研究团队和伦理委员会。

The deployed survey records consent version and timestamp without collecting a name or handwritten signature. If the ethics committee requires an identifiable signature, use a separate consent system and keep identity data physically and logically separated from questionnaire responses.

当前问卷记录同意版本和时间，不收集姓名或手写签名。如果伦理委员会要求可识别签名，应使用独立同意系统，并将身份信息与问卷回答进行物理和逻辑隔离。
